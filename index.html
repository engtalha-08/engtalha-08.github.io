01 About

Business apps that fit how people already work

Hands-on Power Platform development — apps, approvals, data models and reporting — built around real business processes.

I'm a Power Platform developer who enjoys turning real processes — leave requests, approvals, sales pipelines, document handling — into reliable apps and automated flows.

My work covers Canvas Apps and Model-driven Apps, Power Automate cloud and scheduled flows, Power BI dashboards and secure Dataverse data models — including tables, relationships, security roles and Business Process Flows.

I'm especially interested in Dynamics 365 CRM customization: shaping forms, views and dashboards, and extending them with Business Rules, JavaScript and C# plugins where the standard tools run out.
